- (P) <phi|H_eff(r)|phi> = f(phi) + D(phi; r), f(phi) = <s phi|H|s phi>, D = sum_bad sigma (sqrt(r) phi_x - phi_y/sqrt(r))^2 >= 0; E_FN(r) - E* = [f(phi_FN) - E*] + D(phi_FN; r); each self-consistent solve lowers E_FN by >= D (certified descent). Needs only r_xy r_yx >= 1.
- (P) For a sign that is not an eigenstate's sign no interior fixed point exists: the minimiser lies on a face (phi* = 0 on Z, KKT mu_x = (sHs phi*)_x >= 0 on Z); the optimal field diverges on bonds into Z; convergence is sublinear (run data: decrements ~ k^-2.3); E*(s_1) probably well below 4.6e-6 (power-law tail ~3e-7, not trusted).
- (P) The iteration converges monotonically to a KKT value (copositivity problem; not necessarily global). E_FN(s, .) is not convex in log r. The self-consistent loop is majorisation-minimisation (CCCP) on a DC program.
- (P) Error map at an interior optimum: Delta E(delta) = delta^T W_B delta - (B^T W_B delta)^T L_K^{-1} (B^T W_B delta), bounds (1 - rho) delta^T W_B delta <= Delta E <= delta^T W_B delta, rho = lambda_max(L_K^{-1} L_B) < 1 (also the fixed-sign convergence rate); exact bound Delta E <= E_{x~phi*^2}[sum_bad sigma r* (cosh delta - 1)]. Lattice analogue of Caffarel-Pinar-Scemama's attractive boundary: the faces Z where chambers touch; a wrong-chamber attractor appears only through the representation step (fitting a non-self-consistent target).
