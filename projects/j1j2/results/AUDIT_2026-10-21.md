# Audit of the current loop plan against existing results (2026-10-21, read-only)

Paths are relative to `projects/j1j2/`. Energies are per site, dE = E - E0 (6x6 E0 = -0.50380965), unless marked
"relative" (dE/|E0|) or "total". Terms:
- **phi_FN[s,a]:** one-shot FN amplitude, the ground state of the sign-free FN Hamiltonian H_FN built from the guide (s, a).
- **phi\*(s):** the self-consistent FN amplitude for sign s (bond-field fixed point, i.e. the best amplitude for that
  sign). Its energy is the "ceiling".
- **w_s:** wrong-sign weight, sum of |psi0|^2 over configurations with the wrong sign.
- **X_tau:** the expelled ("switched-off") set, phi\*/|psi_P| < tau.
- **KKT flip:** flip x in X where mu_x = s_x (H s phi)_x > 0.
- **psi_P:** the symmetrised ViT (dE 1.32e-4, E_FN 1.02e-4).

## 3. Contradictions between notes
1. "Sign half closes at storable cost" (RESEARCH_MAP 10-20) conflicts with three facts:
   - the KKT labels need the oracle phi\*;
   - the best stored-sign w_s is 2e-5, against 1.2e-6 needed;
   - AVSL shows that unverified network flips raise E_FN.
2. "Flip-all = KKT = oracle" holds at tau = 1e-2. The quoted 9.15e-7 is flip-all at tau = 0.1; KKT gives 9.48e-7.
3. RESEARCH_MAP 3j calls "same-input VMC controls lose energy" the loop's "genuine edge over VMC". Those controls used Adam, or minSR at N << P, so they are not a sound VMC baseline (CLAUDE.md rule 2). At 8x8 a minSR control tied FN: -3.87(2.21) vs -2.84(0.59)e-5.
4. Optimiser guidance conflicts: STATUS 10-03 says SR, "no Adam"; writeback_tail says Adam > minSR.
5. Per-round contraction: the IT2 verdict assumed 0.85-0.89, but stall_6x6 measured 0.58-0.64 for early rounds. The iteration-3 skip rested on the wrong number.
6. Withdrawn DMC numbers are still quoted:
   - the it2_fn step-averaged family (in T3, IT2_VERDICT);
   - paper main.tex: 6x6 -0.503667(63) and 8x8 M=128 values on the uncalibrated estimator;
   - FN populations below E0 are reported in learned_loop/IT2.
7. The AVSL "K1 label" s_x(H s a)_x > 0 is the KKT mu with a in place of phi\*. The "Krylov label is wrong" concession (CLAUDE_REPLY vs AVSL12 reply) compares a global-threshold Krylov arm with a different rule.
8. Pass bars of 1e-5 (bond field, cx_refresh) sit near psi_P + 2 Lanczos (8.6e-6). The equal-hop baseline for a zero-hop guide is psi_P itself (E_FN 1.02e-4). For one hop it is psi_P + 1 Lanczos (E_FN 1.89e-5).

## 4. Pruned list: tests that are new and necessary
1. **(a) as running, with three additions:** a second seed; the decade splice from `cx_reanchor_diag`; and the share of the residual error that lies on X_0.1 and near-node configurations. Pass stays 1e-5 at zero hop. Compare with the equal-hop references above.
2. **Walker-accessible KKT label (new; not in the plan's tests).** On exact 6x6, compute mu_x and X from the fitted amplitude (from 1) instead of phi\*. Measure:
   - precision/recall against the oracle X_0.1, by weight decade;
   - E_FN ceiling of the resulting sign.
   This settles whether step (3) works without the oracle.
3. **Sign net + one exact hop trained on the s_2 labels** (new). Bar: stored w_s <= 2e-6 and ceiling within 1.5x of 9.15e-7. Existing stored nets reach only 2e-5.
4. **(c) only if 1 reaches <= ~3e-5:** SR with N >= P on H_FN[s_2, a] against fixed-sign SR on H at s_2, equal samples, exact E_FN, 3 rounds.
5. **Drop (b) as a campaign.** Replace it with an offline analysis of the saved phi\*(s_2)/(s_3) tables: where the 1.07e-6 residual wrong-sign weight sits, and its mu and Krylov r_s. No new solves.

**Existing results that change the plan:**
- **Majorisation:** SR on H_FN equals fixed-sign VMC to first order.
- **E_FN is set by the amplitude at the nodes:** 9.2e-5 for a refit ViT against 1.95e-5 exact, with the same sign. Spin-only nets fail on the a^2 < 1e-9 tail.
- **KKT labels need phi on configurations that walkers never visit.**
- **Stored-sign precision is ~17x short of s_2.**
- **The exact loop converges** in about 10 rounds from a ViT start. The risk is the representation step, not sign stalling.
