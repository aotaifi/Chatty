# 2026-10-21 J1-J2: ViT refit and T0 verdicts
**What was built.** Two runs of the pre-registered tests on exact 6x6, with the sign s_2 (s_1 flipped on the expelled set):
- the ViT refit to the best amplitude phi*(s_2): refresh_17409419 and refresh2_17409866, two seeds;
- T0, which takes amplitude ratios from PP, RBM+PP, the ViT and the refreshed ViT: t0_17410220.

Both runs live under /project/theorie/a/A.Otaifi/chatty_compress6/runs. The results are in
projects/j1j2/experiments/compress_6x6/README.md and RESEARCH_MAP.md.

**Results.**
- The refit fails: fixed-node error 9.2e-5 against a 1e-5 bar, with both seeds agreeing.
- T0 fires its kill rule: PP's tail displacement eps is 0.78x the ViT's, where the rule asked for a factor 2.
- Every network gives a fixed-node error of about 0.8x its own VMC error. The guide's amplitude ratios set the
  fixed-node energy, not the improved sign.

**Why.** The PI-approved route (a Pfaffian with backflow, times a ViT) rested on the claim that a determinant represents
near-node cancellations natively. T0 tested that claim with no training, and it does not hold for a fixed pairing.

**Rejected.**
- Rescuing T0 through its clause (c), PP x |1 + alpha(E_L - E)|. That correction is a one-hop gain, and psi_P + 1
  Lanczos does better.
- Running T1 straight away. The kill rule fired, and the PI wrote it.

**Running.** T0b, the same diagnostics on the early RBM+PP checkpoints B1 and B2. B2's VMC error is close to the ViT's,
so the comparison is at equal accuracy. It decides whether eps is an architecture property or tracks overall accuracy.

**Three things to understand.**
1. eps is the additive error of a network near a node: delta log a ~ eps/a. The fixed-node error comes from the
   configurations rarer than 1e-10, and there eps must be about 1e-8. All networks sit at 3-6e-7.
2. The splice test: the ViT is precise enough for configurations of probability >= 1e-9. Below 1e-11 lies 60% of the
   fixed-node gap.
3. The flip labels need the exact amplitudes, so the loop's sign step cannot run from a network today.
