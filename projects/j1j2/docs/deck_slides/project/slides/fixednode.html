A bond is blocked when the guide's sign makes its hop positive. f_J is one Jacobi step of the fixed-node equation, computed from the network with one neighbour sum.

Equations from the j1j2 equation board (blocks 4 and 20). FN-GFMC samples the ground state of H_FN; its energy is an upper bound on E0 for any guide. The guide's amplitude enters only through V_psi. f_J(x) = 0 where the network already solves the fixed-node equation at x. One round's exact fixed-node correction is about 1.1-1.3 times the guide's own f_J (eps^2 0.078 round 1, 0.058 round 2; PLAN.md lines 450-455, Tests L and K3, pfaffian_l, pfaffian_k).
