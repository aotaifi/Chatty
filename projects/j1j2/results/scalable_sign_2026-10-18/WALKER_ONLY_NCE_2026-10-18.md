# Walker-only FN-amplitude learning and conservative sign gates — 2026-10-18

## Scientific goal
Develop scalable FN/Krylov sign improvement without exact FN-amplitude labels. This experiment replaces the exact supervised FN teacher in the 20-site pilot with a population FN power-projector, fits the mixed-distribution density ratio, and tests whether its *callable* amplitudes support local sign corrections. This is **a diagnostic, not a scalable 6x6 proof**.

## Correct training distribution
Starting frozen guide signs s(x) are inherited from the prior Krylov experiment. Analytic Jastrow guide g0(x)=exp(-0.30 H_xx) is callable and positive. A first-order importance-sampled FN projector with systematic population resampling yields samples from f(x) ∝ g0(x) phi_FN(x); a separate Metropolis sampler yields q(x)∝g0(x)^2. Train a symmetry-invariant periodic residual CNN with logistic noise-contrastive loss; the logit estimates log[f(x)/q(x)] = log[phi_FN(x)/g0(x)] + constant. The actual amplitude model is `g0(x)*exp(CNN(x))`. **Do not train on the raw mixed-walker histogram as if it were phi_FN^2.**

### Sampler/training (one trained model, seed 53)
- 3,500 FN walkers; 450 burn-in and 450 measurement steps, thin 4; 395,500 recorded positive samples and 65,532 unique states.
- 395,500 reference samples, 131,449 unique states.
- 103,084 cached unique FN projector rows and **8,246,720 Hamiltonian-neighbor probes** for training data (expensive; counts *neighbor probes*, not unique NQS calls).
- 1,200 CNN optimizer steps. Train/held-out class BCE at 1200: 0.615/0.596. Symmetry orbit holdout (diagnostic only).
- Exact FN wavefunction was **not used for training**, selection, or sign gating. Ground truth FN was calculated only in the independent post-hoc audit.
- Remaining large-system limitations: explicit 20-site state->index array, stored sign table, cached diagonal/Jastrow values across basis, FN walker population-control bias, only one network training seed.

### Same-guide independent exact FN validation
The eigenproblem is for **exactly the FN operator generated by the analytic Jastrow guide and frozen Krylov signs**, not the earlier FN guide.

| Metric | Measured value |
|---|---:|
| Exact consistent FN eigenvalue | -9.5245774818 |
| Eigen residual norm | 7.16e-9 |
| FN Rayleigh energy of learned amplitude | **-9.4013837145** |
| FN Rayleigh energy of bare Jastrow | -6.2146696360 |
| Overlap learned amplitude with exact FN | **0.9875299981** |
| Overlap bare Jastrow with exact FN | 0.6085292808 |
| RMS local log-amplitude ratio error vs true FN, 5,606 valid sampled hops | **0.17986 (learned)** vs **0.99380 (Jastrow)** |

These are real positive *amplitude-learning* findings with no exact-amplitude supervised training. However, their generalization to larger N and independent FN populations is unknown.

### Actual sign-correction audit (four candidate-pool seeds 301..304; one trained network)
Candidate pools discovered with local guide sampling and one-hop neighbors; prioritize observed signed couplings; evaluate at most 300 candidate symmetry orbits. Greedy energy gate using only learned amplitude.

**Important correction:** Raw preliminary `guide_predicted_gain` outputs ~10^3 are *unnormalized* Jastrow-squared numerators; they are NOT physical variational energy changes. Evaluate normalized wavefunctions and full H expectation/edge-boundary energy post hoc.

| Fixed amplitude used to independently evaluate selected flips | NN-guided mean delta E | Bare-Jastrow-guided mean delta E |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original Jastrow amplitude | -0.001556 | -0.013928 |
| Prior, **different-guide** FN amplitude | +0.000851 | +0.001016 |
| True FN amplitude **of the same Jastrow FN operator** | **-0.001996** | -0.000138 |

Negative delta E means improvement. In the **same-guide FN audit**, full NN-predicted correction sets improve 2/4 individual trials; two worsen. The prior-guide FN is a transfer check, NOT the same operator and should not be substituted for the consistent ground truth.

Early selections are sometimes shared across sampling seeds, so 4/4 does NOT imply independent evidence at 4 different physical sign environments. Post-hoc k comparison is exploratory and can be cherry-picked; preregister small k on independent seeds/clusters next.

## Suggested next decisive test
1. Repeat NCE training with independent FN walker populations/seeds and confirm stable local-ratio error.
2. Test **small batches (e.g. max 1–2 orbit flips per round)** followed by a new FN solve / walker sampling, versus always taking all predicted positive-gain flips, under a *matched total* probe budget.
3. Replace globally stored s(x) by a callable **composite-sign network**; implement the analytic g0(x) and H_xx directly from spin bitstrings, eliminating 2^N lookup arrays.
4. Track both fixed-amplitude and after-FN-reoptimization energy, sign probability mass, and total neighbor/model calls; ED/FN exact targets evaluation-only.
5. Test larger sizes only after the access/evaluation path is genuinely local.

## Reproducibility
Required Python modules: numpy, scipy, pytorch. Relative to the existing `scalable_sign_2026-10-18` folder, scripts:
- `j1j2_walker_only_amplitude20.py`: generate mixed walkers and g0^2 reference, train CNN by NCE, rank/gate local updates.
- `j1j2_walker_only_amplitude20_energy_audit.py`: normalized external Jastrow/prior FN energy audits.
- `j1j2_walker_only_consistent_fn20.py`: build/diagonalize consistent FN operator and audit actual ratio/energy.
- `j1j2_walker_only_gate_depth20.py`: replay same candidate pools, measure true FN energy by greedy-batch length.
- Matching `*_results.json` record all seeds and evaluated candidates.
- Dependencies: `j1j2_local_query_diagnostic.py`, `j1j2_budgeted_orbit20.py`, `j1j2_neural_guide20.py`; arrays built by `prepare_sign20.py` and `audit_sign20_targets.py`. The exact 20-site targets never train the new NCE network.

**Status:** 20-site proof of FN-amplitude learning from walkers; sign-repair reliability remains mixed, and no large-system scaling claim is warranted.
