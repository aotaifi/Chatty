# Sign gap per site as a size-converging correction: killed (2026-10-29)

**What was tested.** Whether delta(N) = (E0 - E_B)/N, with E_B the ground energy of the sign-free Marshall-frame partner
(J2 spin-flip term made ferromagnetic), converges fast enough in cluster size that E0 = E_B(QMC) + N delta(ED fit) predicts the
J1-J2 (J2 = 0.5) energy at 8x8 / 10x10 to 1e-4 per site. Plan and frozen kill test: projects/j1j2/PLAN_signgap.md.

**What was built.** A worker wrote a symmetry-adapted Lanczos code (translations, C4, spin flip; sectors k = 0 and (pi,pi)) and
computed exact E0 and E_B on seven periodic clusters, N = 16, 18, 20, 26, 32, 34, 36, at J2 = 0, 0.3, 0.5
(/scratch/a/A.Otaifi/ansatz_runs/signgap/A0_ed/, about 24 core-hours). Checks: J2 = 0 gives E_B = E0 to 1e-13; 6x6 E0 matches
the published value to 4e-9; an independent no-symmetry code agrees at N <= 20.

**Result.** Fitting on N <= 32 and predicting 34 and 36, delta misses by 1.5e-3 to 1.1e-2 per site, the same as fitting E0
directly (ratio 0.99 to 1.39; the bar was 1e-4 and a 5x gain). It fails equally at J2 = 0.3, where both models are Neel.

**Rejected alternatives.** Running the QMC stage anyway (200 core-hours): pointless once the small-cluster fit misses by 50x.
Using square clusters only: just 4x4 and 6x6 are exact, so there is no holdout, and the two-point forms already differ by
1.6e-4 at 8x8.

**Three things to understand.**
1. With the same fit, the delta route misses by r0 - r_B and the direct route by r0 (Astra's identity). The method needs the
   two finite-size residuals to be correlated.
2. They are not: E_B/N is smooth in N (residual ~2e-4), while the frustrated E0/N jumps by up to 1e-2 between cluster shapes.
   So delta inherits all of E0's scatter.
3. The cause is not the phase at J2 = 0.5 (the risk named beforehand): it fails at 0.3 too. The scatter is a property of the
   frustrated sign structure that the sign-free partner lacks.
