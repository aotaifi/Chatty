J1–J2 fixed-node loop4
One round: the guide is a stored sign times the network amplitude (1); the target multiplies the amplitude by its own Jacobi factor (2); the network is refitted to the target, label-free, walkers only for E' and c (3); the sign of one Lanczos step is stored in the sign network (4). The final energy is FN-GFMC with one Lanczos step on the guide (p = 1). Walker cost stays one level per round because the round lives in the network, not in nested steps.
