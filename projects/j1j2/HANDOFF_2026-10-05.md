## Established (all in the paper unless noted)
- Krylov sign step s' = s sgn[T - r], T = energy-optimal cut ("no flip" included, never raises energy).
  Exact 4x4 amplitude: exact signs in 3-4 steps (J2/J1 0.4-1.0). 6x6 ViT amplitude vs our 6x6 ED
  (E0/N = -0.50380965): w_s 2.0e-2 (Marshall) -> 1.1e-3 -> 2.3e-4; ViT signs 1.3e-4 (and a Krylov fixed point).
- Exact FN/Krylov loop: monotone (E_FN[new] <= <H>[new guide] <= E_FN[old]), reaches the ground state;
  Anderson (m=3) ~3x faster; iterations to eps<1e-6 flat from 16 to 36 sites (82-104 plain, 32-57 Anderson).
- Sampled 4x4 (histogram amplitude): energy floor ~1/N; real walkers ~3x less efficient than iid.
- Untrained iteration, ViT amplitude + Krylov -> FN (M=128): 6x6 -0.503100(40) (76 populations),
  8x8 -0.498210(25); both ~6e-4/site above the ViT (6x6 -0.503654(21), 8x8 -0.498823(35)). FN bound verified.
- Learned loop: 4x4 network on frozen <H_FN> with fresh samples iterates stably; 6x6 one learned iteration
  E_FN -0.503667(63) = ViT within error, w_s 8e-5 (< ViT). 8x8 MLE+SR updates failed (ledger).
  VMC continuation of the ViT for 9 GPU-h changes nothing (-1(14)e-5).
- Sign recursion fix (not yet in paper): stored sign net trained on tempered samples |a|^{2 beta}
  (beta 0.5) PLUS one-hop neighbours, then ONE exact Krylov hop -> matches the exact 4x4 loop
  (results/stored_signs/, stored_signs/capacity/, sign_design_tests/). "Self-sealing": FN suppresses
  a(x) on wrong-sign states, so |a|^2 samples never see the flips.
- Depth from Marshall with exact amplitude grows slowly with N (exact signs: 3 -> 6 steps, 16 -> 36 sites;
  w_s<1e-6: 3 steps for 20-36). r - r_FN is identically 0 (FN identity) — dead idea.
