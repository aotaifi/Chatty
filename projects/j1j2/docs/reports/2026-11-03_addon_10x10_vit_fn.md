# Fixed-node (p=0) barely improves the published 10x10 ViT: the add-on test A stops at this step

- **Result:** FN-GFMC p=0 from our JAX copy of the published 10x10 ViT (nqs-models j1j2_square_10x10_05, symm_trxy_ising),
  J2=0.5 PBC: E_mix/N = -0.497682(4), QUOTABLE, 2 chains (-0.497681(5), -0.497683(6), chi2/dof 0.03); growth estimator
  -0.497679(3). The guide's own VMC is -0.497677(5) (2 chains). Gain 5(6)e-6, consistent with zero at 1 sigma.
- **Against the bar:** best published bound -0.4976939(2) (arXiv 2603.14425 Table 1); FN is 1.2e-5 (3 sigma) above it.
  Pre-registered branch (vit10_runs/a05/ANALYSIS_BRIEF.md): mean + 2 sigma > -0.497684 -> A stops at affordable cost.
- **Prediction missed:** lead predicted -0.49775..-0.49770 (gain 2-7e-5), scaling the 8x8 RBM+PP FN gain (8.3e-5).
  Reading (hypothesis): FN p=0 repairs amplitudes except across sign-violating bonds; the ViT's amplitude is already good,
  so its remaining error is in its sign (nodes), which p=0 cannot touch. The RBM+PP guide at 8x8 had worse amplitudes.
- **Checks:** driver gfmc_guide.py (cd322f3) agrees with exact FN at 6x6 (-0.07 sigma), broken V_sf misses by 31 sigma,
  matches the launched 8x8 code to 4.6e-14; f32h + f64 recheck passed a held-out precision test (bias 3e-10/site).
  Raw block series: figures/2026-11-03_a05_blocks.png (burn 3 tau removed, running means flat, both chains overlap).
- **Cost:** ~16 A40-h for the pilot (+ ~6 A40-h A0.2-A0.4); sizing estimate (error ~5e-6 two-chain) held.
- **What would still move the 10x10 number (not run):** changing the sign. One signed Lanczos step on this guide costs
  ~225x more evaluations per walker step (neighbours of neighbours x 64 symmetry passes), out of reach here; a cheap
  stored sign at 10x10 does not exist yet (sign angle).
