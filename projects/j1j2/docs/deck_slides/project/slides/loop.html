Readout

S: sign network. B: pair-product network. The copy needs no labels: the target is exact at every sampled configuration.

One round: the guide is a stored sign times the network amplitude (1); the target multiplies the amplitude by its own Jacobi factor (2); the network is refitted to the target, label-free, walkers only for E' and c (3); the sign of one Lanczos step is stored in the sign network (4). The final energy is FN-GFMC with one Lanczos step on the guide (p = 1). Walker cost stays one level per round because the round lives in the network, not in nested steps. Sources: PLAN.md lines 2-3 (goal), 458-471 (label-free round, stored sign), board blocks 20, 25, 27. c* = 3.9-4.0 everywhere (PLAN_pfaffian.md).
