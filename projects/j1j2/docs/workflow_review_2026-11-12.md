## Deliverable 2: my view

**What works.** The evidence machinery works. Numbers in delta_today.md carry job ids and errors. The quotable gate,
VERDICT lines and watchers catch real errors. The duplicate-chain catch moved a headline from 3.3 sigma to 2.7 sigma
(LESSONS:33), and the two retrained seeds agree to 0.2 sigma. Astra reviews catch errors before they cost compute:
five overclaims on 10-28, the T-BOND surrogate rejected before running, and the reweighting counterexample on 11-12. The
angle split is also right: the sign and Pfaffian questions are genuinely separate and each has a plan.

**What costs the PI attention.** The PI is the project's step-back trigger and its mechanism auditor. Every turn
of the last two weeks came from a PI question: storage on 10-27, "stop and explain" on 10-28, "am I jumping ahead?" on
11-12, and "the optimiser is a known function" on 11-12. Each one was answered with more rule text, the last in three
files within an hour. More text has not moved the trigger off the PI. The countable step-back never fired (row 27),
and the two-week look the PI set was never run (row 28). I think rules written as prose cannot do this job. They get
read once at session start, diluted by ~685 other lines, and then the most recent number decides the next action.
Root §4 already says it: "These checks belong in a script, not in your attention." The evidence rules work because
they are scripts. The thinking rules fail because they are prose.

**What costs the project time.** Predictions are now made, which is real progress since 10-28. But a miss is still
absorbed rather than diagnosed. On 11-12 the base arm was predicted at -0.49892..-0.49896 and measured at -0.4988780(27),
a miss of ~4e-5, about 15 sigma. The next actions were a new run (the p=1 arm) and "re-predict R1". In delta_today.md and
PLAN.md (Motivation Q8) I found the miss restated as a trend ("FN p=0 shrinking with net quality"), not a named term. I
did not read the equation board, so a term may be there. Point 1 says a miss is the result. In practice a miss is still a
reason to run the next arm. The lead also spends hours on placement and the shared 3-GPU cap: four delta entries
between 11:40 and 11:55 on 11-12 moved jobs between slices. That is mechanics, and it is fine for the lead, but it
competes with derivation for the same context.

**Where I disagree.**
- *With the PI:* adding each critique to the project file, LESSONS and a skill makes the stack worse, not the lead
  better. One home per rule, and the enforcement in the job path (change 2).
- *With the lead:* about 8 parallel threads under a rule that says one open question. Either point 4 is wrong (I think
  it is too strict, and the PI's 11-11 rule is better) or the practice is. Today the file and the practice disagree,
  and nobody said so.
- *With the agreement itself:* point 5 still sets the goal's number at 10x10+, but the PI moved the goal to "at >= 8x8"
  on 11-04 (row 13). FOUNDATIONS.md should be retired, not enforced (row 24).
