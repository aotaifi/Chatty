# Fable consultant on the bond-field FN idea (2026-10-19, read-only review)
Consultant opinion, not instructions. Key points (full text relayed in the session):
- The self-consistent optimum is a node-placement (membership) field, not a smooth ratio field: the minimiser of <a|sHs|a> over a >= 0 sits on the orthant boundary; configurations where the sign s is wrong are expelled (a*_x = 0, r_xy -> infinity, r_yx -> 0). The 6x6 c-iteration gaps shrink geometrically at 0.84/solve; Aitken limit ~3e-6/site for s_1.
- Field errors: bulk bad bonds second order (weight |h| phi_x phi_y); bonds into near-node configurations that stay in the support cheap; bonds into expelled configurations FIRST order, full weight - where ViT ratios lose. Expulsion is a per-configuration classification ("is s wrong at x") = the next sign step, storable at fixed cost (sign net, fact 5).
- Extra expressiveness of reciprocal fields never helps at the optimum (H_eff - sHs is PSD bond by bond); the optimum beats |psi0| ratios because it pays nothing on expelled configurations.
- Cost: a one-hop bond field costs n_bad (~N) extra hops per walker step -> not viable at scale; the field must be zero-hop: ViT ratios + a veto bit.
- Literature: van Bemmel 1994, ten Haaf 1995, Sorella-Capriotti 2000 (FN-gamma), Capriotti et al. 2001, LRDMC (Casula 2005), CP-AFQMC self-consistency (Qin-Shi-Zhang 2016); no known lattice work optimising the wall field directly or noting the expulsion structure (not web-verified).
- Decisive test: X_tau = {x: phi*_x/|psi_P(x)| < tau}; E_FN(s_1, ViT ratios on the support, a = 0 on X_tau) and the three-class gap decomposition; <= 1e-5 -> train the veto as a sign net; > 3e-5 -> bulk-ratio (amplitude) problem. Second check: flip s_1 on X_tau and compute E_FN(s_2, ViT ratios) - if it drops, the expelled set is the next sign step at fixed cost and the loop closes without storing phi.
