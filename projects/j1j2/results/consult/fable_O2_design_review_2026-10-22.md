# O2 design review (Fable, 2026-10-22, read-only). Tags: [M] measured from raw files, [C] read in code, [L] literature, [I] inference.
Paths: cx = experiments/compress_6x6; runs = /project/theorie/a/A.Otaifi/Chatty/runs/compress6/runs. Energies per site above E0.

**Verdict: as written, O2 most likely ends in an ambiguous STALL, and its PASS bar is nearly unreachable for a reason that is not the loop.** [M] The fit tax alone is 6.1e-6: the best tail-trained fit of the exact phi*(s_2) plus one hop gives 7.06e-6 against the ceiling 9.15e-7 (g1_17426232). The 1e-5 bar therefore leaves 3e-6 for everything else (loop not yet converged, moving target, 40-min fits, alpha by the O1 rule instead of the exact E_FN). [M] O1 has not run (no o1_* under runs), so step (a) has no rule yet. [M] Cost per round with PP+backflow at T1s-2 speed: fit 2400 s + table 5782 s + 13 solves ~1500 s = 2.7 h, so 4 rounds + round 0 = ~12 h, above the 8 A40-h budget (t1s2_ppbf_17418857 table_sec, g1 scan 1370 s per 12 solves). Add the fit-free exact chain (point 5) and the test becomes diagnosable at +1.5 h; without it, keep the stop rule but do not read a STALL as a loop property.

**(1) Contraction or spurious fixed point.** [I, from the consult's own (P) lines] With an exact fit and alpha by the O1 rule the chain is monotone: E_FN[g_{k+1}] <= <H>_{hop(phi_k)} <= <H>_{phi_k} = E_FN[g_k] (FN is below its guide's energy; alpha = 0 is in the O1 search; phi_k is the FN state of g_k). The map hop∘T has a fixed point at or above the ceiling, not at phi* (phi* is not an eigenstate, so hop(phi*) != phi*); that is harmless. The fit breaks the middle inequality, and the error that matters is the systematic one at the walls, not the rms: [M] the T1s-2 fits are biased LOW in the deep tail (mean log error at decades -13/-14: ppbf -0.038/-0.093, pp -0.035/-0.091; the ViT +0.28 at -14), and [M] 2.45% of orbits of the fitted ppbf+hop sit at phi*/|g| < 0.1 (X_frac, g1). [I] A low a_y at a deep y raises the FN wall diagonal at y (sum_bad |H_yx| a_x/a_y) and lowers it at its neighbours, so the next solve pushes phi_k lower at y, the next fit learns the lower value, and y is expelled. Expelling a wrong-signed configuration is free; expelling a right-signed one costs energy by the consult's excision bound. This is exactly "fitting a non-self-consistent target" (math consult 2026-10-19 line 8), so yes, it bites here, and it bites harder than for phi* because every round re-fits its own expulsions. Fix: per round, record the set {phi_k/|g_k| < 0.1} split by |psi0|^2 weight into s_2-right and s_2-wrong signed (the oracle is free at 6x6), and the per-decade MEAN of log a_{k+1} - log phi_k (cx_t1s decade_table already returns mean_count; only rms is quoted). A growing right-signed expelled weight is the spurious attractor; a flat one is not.

**(2) Four rounds.** [M] The 0.6-0.65 reference is the first own solves from a fitted ViT at 9.2e-5 (refresh_17409419: 9.16 -> 4.30 -> 2.55 -> 1.69e-5, rates 0.47, 0.59, 0.66); from psi_P the exact loop ran 0.64, 0.75, 0.78 rising to 0.87 by ~1e-5 (stall_6x6), and plain iteration near phi* contracts 0.92 (AUDIT_2026-10-21). [I] O2 starts at 2.37e-5 (PP one-hop, exact alpha), where the measured rate is already ~0.66 and rises ~0.07 per round: 4 exact, fit-free rounds give ~0.41, i.e. 9.7e-6, marginal even with zero fit tax. The hop∘T map may contract faster than T alone (unmeasured). So 4 rounds is fair only for the exact chain and not for the fitted one; the honest budget is "the exact chain's round count to 1e-5, plus the rounds the fit tax costs". Fix: measure the exact chain first (point 5) and set the round budget from it; define c_k on E_FN - 9.15e-7, since c_k -> 1 near the ceiling by construction.

**(3) Start and arms.** VMC PP with zero backflow is the right oracle-free start [I]. A ViT-guided arm adds nothing: [M] tail-trained ViT one-hop 1.52e-5 and plain ViT one-hop 2.90e-5, both above PP's 2.37e-5 at equal hop (g1, t0phys). Damping slows a map that is already too slow (wrong direction) [I]; Anderson/extrapolation is well founded for the exact map ([M] stall_6x6: overshoot t = 1.25 still lowers <H>; RESEARCH_MAP: Anderson ~3x faster but unsafe from bad starts) but cannot remove a fit tax that already exceeds the remaining budget, so it belongs after the exact chain shows the rounds are the bottleneck, not the fit. One cheap arm I would add: the same loop with the exact phi_k as target (no network), which is point 5.

**(4) Self-healing DMC.** [L, read sections I-III of arXiv:2609.01301 (Caffarel, Pinar, Scemama 2026) and the abstract plus passages of arXiv:0808.3417 (Reboredo, Hood, Kent 2009)] Noise-free SHDMC = project the FN solution onto a finite basis by an unweighted chi^2 (their eq. 18), then re-solve: the same two steps as O2 (b)-(c). On their model the FN energy rises monotonically for every basis size, converging to a wrong node even at N = 10 where the start agrees with the exact energy to 10 digits (their Table 1: bias 1.9e-2 at N = 4, 1.7e-9 at N = 10); the attractive fixed point's stability index eta -> 1 as N grows, so convergence slows as capacity rises. Their partial cure is to restrict the chi^2 to the nodal region, and they argue a localized basis is needed so the node can move without disturbing the bulk. RHK's convergence argument rests on sampling noise acting as simulated annealing ("random errors can be used to eliminate systematic errors"); O2 uses exact solves, so it is precisely the noiseless case Caffarel analyses. O2 already weights the walls (decade stratification, bond term LAM_B [C]), which is the Caffarel cure in spirit, and the 3x3 backflow is the "local distortion"; what O2 reproduces is the failure signature: c_k > 1 is possible and the design does not name it. Fix: a RISE verdict (E_FN(g_{k+1}) > E_FN(g_k) by more than solve tolerance), reported separately from STALL, and the fit's bond-term share of the loss per decade logged, since a rising energy with a falling value loss is their mechanism.

**(5) Cheapest change for a diagnosable STALL.** Run, beside the fitted loop, the fit-free reference chain phi_{k+1} = T(hop(phi_k)) from the same start (no table, no training: ~13 solves per round at ~115 s [M], ~1.5 h for 4 rounds). Per round report: E_FN(exact chain), E_FN(fitted chain), and the fit tax = E_FN(hop(a_{k+1})) - E_FN(hop(phi_k)) with the splice (fitted >= -9, exact below). Then STALL reads one of three ways: exact chain also > 0.9: the map's rate (budget, not representation); fit tax >= the exact round's gain: representation floor; fit tax small but right-signed expelled weight rising: spurious attractor. Without the reference chain these three give the same two numbers.

Not checked: the hop∘T contraction rate (never run), alpha_V vs alpha_FN (O1 unrun), seed scatter (one seed everywhere), and whether a 40-min warm fit reaches T1s-2 quality on a moving target (T1s-2 needed 151 steps = 3 h from a T1s warm start).
