# Handoff 2026-10-26: Test L8 (one loop round at 8x8) closed

**What was built.** A walker-based refit for the J1-J2 loop at 8x8 (projects/j1j2/experiments/gfmc_fn_lattice/l8/):
the FN-GFMC walkers of the guide g_0 = (RBM+PP network) x (one Lanczos hop) are dumped, and the same network class is
refitted to the FN solution phi_0 by maximising the fidelity, using walker samples and VMC samples only. Then the new
network plus a hop would be the next guide. Controls (bitwise walk, exact 4x4 overlaps, wrong-sign weights, zero-step
identity) all passed.

**Result.** The refit learns nothing that holds on an independent walker chain: held-out fidelity falls from step 1.
Report: projects/j1j2/docs/reports/2026-10-26_l8_loop_round_8x8.md.

**Why (the cause, checked exactly at 4x4).** A network at its VMC energy optimum has zero fidelity gradient toward its own
one-hop state: the gradient is alpha times the energy gradient. So the refit can only absorb what the walkers add beyond
the hop, which at 8x8 is 2.3e-5 per site and is below sampling noise.

**Alternatives rejected.**
- More walker data: the cosine between two chains' gradients stayed at 0 with 8x more VMC samples; and even a perfect refit
  is bounded by 2.3e-5/site per round, under a tenth of the gap to DMRG.
- Larger learning rate / longer refit: overfits from step 1 (train rises, held-out falls as a mirror image).
- Held-out blocks from the same chain: they read high (+2.6 sigma at 4x4) and would have hidden the failure; replaced by
  an independent chain.

**Three things to understand.**
1. The stationarity identity: d/dtheta of fidelity(psi_theta, (1 + alpha(H - E)) psi_0) = alpha dE/dtheta. At an energy
   optimum this is zero, so a same-class network cannot store a hop of itself.
2. The 4x4 positive control started from an under-trained network (30 L-BFGS steps), so it had a large hop gradient. A
   control must start in the same regime as the production case (here: at the energy optimum).
3. Two-hop targets do have a gradient ((H - E)^2 term), which is why the earlier 6x6 refit moved; its loss came from the
   near-node tail, a different cause.
