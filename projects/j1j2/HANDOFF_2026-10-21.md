## Where we stand (plain words)
- Goal: a loop (fixed node <-> sign flip, stored in networks) that improves a trained network's ground state at constant cost per round, scales to 12x12+ and beats brute-force NQS in GPU-h (or reaches accuracy VMC cannot).
- Proven on exact 6x6: with EXACT amplitudes the loop converges (no stall; from the ViT 8.8e-6/site in 12 rounds; from Marshall 1e-6 relative). Flipping the configurations fixed node switches off (expelled set / KKT rule) lowers the FN ceiling 2.51e-6 -> 9.15e-7; precision ~1 by |psi0|^2 weight, recall 0.83. The exact joint loop can only settle on the ground state (math review).
- The one blocker: an amplitude network precise on RARE near-node configurations (|psi|^2 < 1e-9 at 6x6). Every log-space network (ViT, CNN, small correction nets) fails there: additive error law delta log a ~ eps/a (displaced node). Seven constant-cost storage designs failed for this; cause understood (frustration -> sign walls -> non-local amplitudes; local at J2 = 0).
- Current route (PI-approved 2026-10-21): node-aware amplitude network = |det/Pf of a pairing matrix F(x)| x exp(ViT): F = pairing f (warm from our PP or the Z2 Dirac spin-liquid mean field) + LOCAL backflow (moves the nodes), ViT as smooth Jastrow. Fast Woodbury updates make all neighbour ratios cheap (extra hops no longer explode). See results/consult/fable_nqs_architecture_2026-10-21.md (+ physics addendum).
