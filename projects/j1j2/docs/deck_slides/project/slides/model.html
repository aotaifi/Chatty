Model and question

Square lattice, periodic L × L, J2/J1 = 0.5: frustrated, no sign-free basis.

Can a neural network store the fixed-node correction near the nodes, so that the loop beats brute-force neural states at 10 × 10 and beyond, or repairs other groups' networks?

The question as set by the PI (projects/j1j2/CLAUDE.md, viability rule; PLAN.md lines 2-4 goal). Viability means one of three things: a result at sizes where others have none, cheaper than brute-force NQS at equal accuracy, or an add-on that repairs other people's networks.
